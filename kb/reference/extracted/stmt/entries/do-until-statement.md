DO UNTIL Statement
Executes statements in a DO loop repetitively until a condition is true.

Valid in:        DATA step
Categories:      CAS
                 Control
Type:            Executable




                 Syntax
                 DO UNTIL (expression);
                 ...more SAS statements...

                 END;


                 Arguments
                 (expression)
                    is any SAS expression, enclosed in parentheses. You must specify at least one
                    expression.
