Define and demonstrate a reversible interchange contract for main.json and empty.json. These are synthetic decoded source envelopes, not SAS binary datasets. Use Python 3.11+ standard library only; no Parquet or SAS reader is supplied. Keep id as text, amounts numeric or missing, and event_day as raw SAS day counts with the supplied units. Preserve every row, order, source byte and descriptor property. The consumer includes base R character columns: NUL and undecodable text must use an explicitly marked raw-hex fallback, not replacement text or dropped rows. Ordinary representable text stays decoded, including CR/LF and trailing spaces.

Deliver contract.md, report.md and bundle.py. CLI: python3 bundle.py build source.json NEW_DIRECTORY; python3 bundle.py check source.json DIRECTORY. Build data.csv with ordered columns row_no,id,text,amount,event_day. Numeric missing cells and text fallback cells are empty. metadata.json must contain source_sha256, data_sha256, descriptor (unchanged source descriptor), and rows with row_no, text_mode (decoded or raw), text_raw_hex, and amount_missing (null for nonmissing, otherwise the original tag). Positions are the supplied unique row_no, not business id. Reject unsupported encoding, text wider than its descriptor, duplicate row_no or an existing destination before writing outputs. Check must validate both data and metadata against the supplied source, including all associations. Include actual tests and limits in report.md. Do not claim that CSV demonstrates Parquet preservation.
